# Regulatory scope

The project covers the Basel FRTB Standardised Approach in MAR20–MAR23 and Internal Models Approach in MAR30–MAR33.

The reporting context is an India-based trading book with INR as reporting currency. Regulatory parameters represent the Basel global minimum standard. They do not claim to reproduce RBI national discretions, reporting templates or supervisory approval decisions.

The calculation stops at FRTB market-risk capital and Market RWA. The broader Basel output floor is a total-RWA calculation and is not represented as a standalone market-risk floor.
